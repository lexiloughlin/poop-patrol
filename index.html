How to play

Poop pellets are 10 points. Clear them all to finish the cage. Hay bundles give your pig the zoomies. Vacuums get scared and you can bonk them for big points. Robot vacuums chase you. Touching one costs a heart. Lone Star coins only show up for Masaki. Grab one for a 5-second Capital Bonus: every poop pellet is worth triple.

Arrow keys or WASD to move · swipe or use the pad on phones · P to pause
